# FINAL HOSPITAL WORKFLOW

## Workflows
- **OPD (Outpatient Department)**: Streamlined queuing, vitals, consultation, and billing.
- **IPD (Inpatient Department)**: Admission, bed management, nursing rounds, discharge summary, and final settlement.
- **Emergency**: Rapid triage, casualty management, and stabilization before formal admission.
- **Pharmacy**: Inventory management, prescription dispensing, and expiry tracking.
- **Lab**: Sample collection, test processing, verification, and automated result reporting.
- **Billing**: Centralized invoicing, payments, and void management with clinical justification.
