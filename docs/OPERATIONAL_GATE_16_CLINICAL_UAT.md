# Operational Gate 16: Clinical & Statutory BMDC/DGHS User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

## 1. Context & Purpose
Before full clinical deployment across Onnesha Hospital and Diagnostic Center in Bogura, formal verification and sign-off must be completed by the Clinical Director and Hospital Administration in compliance with:
- **BMDC (Bangladesh Medical and Dental Council)** Code of Ethics
- **DGHS (Directorate General of Health Services)** Hospital Licensing Guidelines
- **Drugs Act & National Drug Policy of Bangladesh**

---

## 2. Clinical UAT Checkpoints & Sign-off Matrix

| Area | Verification Criteria | Clinical Sign-off |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Emergency Casualty** | RED/YELLOW/GREEN triage categorization, rapid bed assignment, vitals logging | [ ] Clinical Director |
| **Outpatient (OPD)** | Doctor consultation queue, ICD-10 diagnostic coding, bilingual digital prescriptions | [ ] Chief Medical Officer |
| **Inpatient (IPD)** | Ward/Cabin/ICU bed allocation, nursing vitals chart, OT scheduling, discharge summary | [ ] Nursing Superintendent |
| **Pharmacy POS** | Brand to generic drug substitution rules, batch/expiry alerts, narcotic double-check | [ ] Chief Pharmacist |
| **Diagnostic & LIS** | Reference ranges for adult/pediatric, panic value alerts, hematology/biochemistry validation | [ ] Consultant Pathologist |
| **Billing & Cashier** | Accurate package tariffs, doctor referral commission split, cash drawer reconciliation | [ ] Finance Director |

---

## 3. Statutory Sign-off Protocol
Upon completing dry-run drills across all departments, print the acceptance sign-off document from the OHMS administrative portal and archive the physical signed copy alongside DGHS hospital renewal files.
